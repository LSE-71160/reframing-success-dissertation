# reframing-success-dissertation

Reframing Success: Perceptions of Life After Incarceration in California
Replication materials for an MSc Social Research Methods dissertation submitted to the London School of Economics and Political Science (2026). Candidate 71160.

Study overview

This study examines how reframing success beyond recidivism shapes public evaluation of formerly incarcerated individuals in California. It combines a between-subjects survey experiment (n=300) with 15 semi-structured qualitative interviews. The survey tested whether presenting success through a holistic scorecard rather than a recidivism-only metric shifts public perceptions, policy support, and deservingness beliefs.

Repository contents

Diss_Survey_Analysis.R: R script for all quantitative analyses including ANOVA, linear regression, interaction models, and ordinal logistic regression robustness checks.

Survey_Instrument.pdf: PDF version of the full survey instrument displaying all three vignette conditions for transparency purposes. Note that in the actual survey, participants were randomly assigned to one condition and saw only that version.
